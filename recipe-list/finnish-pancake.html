Pannukakku (Finnish oven pancake)

Pannukakku is a Finnish take on a pancake that is prepared in the oven. A food blogger said it is something in between a pancake, crepe, french toast and custard and that is enough to buy me in. I am excited to make it.

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 3/4 teaspoon of salt
  • 2 1/2 cups of whole milk
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 4 tablespoons of unsalted butter

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 200C. Place the butter in a baking dish and put in over to melt and be bubbly.
  2. Whisk together 4 eggs.
  3. Add in flour, milk, salt and sugar. Whisk until batter is smooth.
  4. Pour the batter slowly over the pan.
  5. Cook for 28-30 mins. It will be golden brown around edges and bubble up over the sides!
